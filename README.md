## The rant

> I scanned ONE avocado and the self-checkout screamed 'UNEXPECTED ITEM IN THE BAGGING AREA' like I'd smuggled a live animal. I AM the bagging area. I live here now. The attendant strolls over at the speed of a glacier, the queue is judging me, and the avocado just sits there, innocent, while a robot accuses me of theft in front of strangers.

342 characters, one rant about the self-checkout machine.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3) writes exactly that rant to `/output/entry.txt` — no
trailing newline, valid UTF-8, 1–400 characters as the gate requires.
Deterministic: two runs write byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
